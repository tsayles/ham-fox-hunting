## Pre-Hunt Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Fox transmitter deployed and confirmed on the air on 147.42 MHz
  (listen for K7LED CW ID and tone sequence)
- [ ] Fox hider confirms hide is within the Sammamish River corridor
  or open-field section of the park (not near concert stage area)
- [ ] 147.42 MHz confirmed clear of other simplex traffic
- [ ] Hunt start and end times confirmed: 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM
- [ ] Participants briefed on concert / hard-stop at 2:30 PM
- [ ] Check-in procedure confirmed: call NCS on 147.42 MHz with
  callsign and time when fox is found
- [ ] Hint policy confirmed: one geographic hint at 30 minutes, one
  directional hint at 60 minutes if needed
- [ ] Finish log ready (paper or phone) for callsigns and times
- [ ] Parking lot or gazebo designated as NCS operating position

## Opening Script — 12:00 PM

> Read on 147.42 MHz at noon. Make sure the fox is on the air before
> starting.

---

*"[NCS callsign], net control for the Mike and Key fox hunt.*

*Good afternoon, everyone. Welcome to Marymoor Park.*

*The hunt is now open. The fox is transmitting on one-four-seven
point four two megahertz simplex. The fox callsign is Kilo Seven
Lima Echo Delta — K7LED. Listen for a fifteen-second tone sequence
followed by the CW ID.*

*The hunt will run until two-thirty PM. You have two and a
half hours.*

*When you find the fox, call me on one-four-seven point four two
with your callsign and your time. I will acknowledge and record
your finish.*

*Please keep your search within the main park area — the wooded
river corridor and open fields to the east. Avoid the concert
venue and stage area on the south end of the park.*

*I will give a check-in and time update every thirty minutes,
starting at twelve-thirty. If you want a hint, call me and
I will do my best.*

*Any questions before we begin?*

*[Pause briefly for questions.]*

*Good luck, and seventy-three. [NCS callsign], net control,
standing by on one-four-seven point four two."*

## Net Control Notes — Marymoor-Specific

- **Concert timing is the hard constraint.** Marymoor Live concert
  gates open at 4 PM. NCS must close the hunt and confirm all
  participants are out of the field no later than 2:30 PM.
- **Light rail access.** Some participants may arrive by
  2 Line (East Link) to the Marymoor Village station. Confirm
  the meeting point is accessible from the transit stop.
- **Park size.** Marymoor is 640 acres. For a milliwatt fox a
  defined search boundary is essential — announce it clearly
  in the opening and repeat it on each check-in.
- **Milliwatt power.** Effective range for the MF-15 at park
  power is roughly 100–400 m depending on foliage. Remind new
  participants that a quiet S-meter is normal at distance; they
  are not missing the signal.
- **Fox recovery.** After closing, coordinate with the fox hider
  on a timeline for recovery before the park fills for the
  concert.
