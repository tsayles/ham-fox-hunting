## ARDF Build Event (Thu 6/26)

**Location:** Wagon Wheel group campsite
**Lead:** Tom KE4HET + M&K volunteers
**Audience:** Licensed amateur radio operators at Radio Camp (1–10)
**Goal:** Participants build working RDF equipment for use at the
weekend demonstration

### Projects (in priority order)
1. **2-meter tape measure Yagi** — required
2. **Step attenuator** — if time and budget allow

### Key Logistics
- All tools transported to site by instructor/volunteers
- Power at site is limited — minimize AC tool requirements
- Target: participants leave with a working, tested antenna
- See `build-event-syllabus.md` for full step-by-step instructions,
  BOMs, tools list, and time estimates

## ARDF Demonstration (Sat–Sun 6/28–6/29)

**Location:** Battery Grattan (visitor tent) ↔ Parade Grounds (fox)
**Audience:** General public — no ham experience assumed

### Setup
- **Fox (Tx):** One of the club's Byonics fox transmitters (MF-15 or
  MF-PC), hidden on the south side of the Parade Grounds
- **Visitor station (Rx):** At the Battery Grattan visitor tent
- **Loaner equipment:** Built antennas + club handhelds loaned to
  visitors to find the fox

### Flow
1. Visitor arrives at Battery Grattan tent
2. Demonstrator explains fox hunting in 1–2 minutes
3. Visitor is loaned a handheld + Yagi (or built antenna)
4. Visitor uses the visitor info sheet to find the fox
5. Fox is re-hidden as needed between groups

### Open items (see issue #2)
- Frequency for demo (coordinate with FD ops)
- Number of loaner setups available
- Guided vs. self-guided format
- Comms between fox hide and visitor tent
