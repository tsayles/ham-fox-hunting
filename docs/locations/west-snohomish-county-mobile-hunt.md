## 2. Hunt area — geographic boundaries

The mobile course is confined to **western Snohomish County**, bounded
by:

| Boundary   | Landmark / route                              |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **North**  | South city limits of Marysville               |
| **East**   | State Route 9 (SR-9)                          |
| **South**  | Snohomish / King county line                  |
| **West**   | Puget Sound shoreline                         |

This window encompasses Everett, Mukilteo, Lynnwood, Edmonds, Brier,
Mountlake Terrace, and the Mill Creek / Silver Lake corridor west of
SR-9. It provides ~15–25 miles of drivable terrain with a variety of
urban-edge, suburban, and light-industrial RF environments — all
reachable without leaving Snohomish County.

**Course concept:** Teams start at a TBD southern/central launch point
within the hunt area and take mobile bearings as they converge on the
hidden transmitter. The final leg is a short on-foot segment at the
finish location, which will be disclosed to registered participants on
hunt day.

## 3. Finish location

The finish location is **within the announced hunt area** and will be
disclosed to registered participants on hunt day. A specific site has
not yet been selected.

Key criteria for finish-location scouting:

- Public access with adequate parking for arriving teams.
- Suitable for a short on-foot final leg to the hidden fox.
- Space for a social wrap-up / scoring check-in after the hunt.
- Restrooms nearby.

## 6. Transmitter / frequency plan

Based on current program equipment status (see
`docs/reports/2026-06-07-fox-transmitter-status.md`).

| Field         | Value                                          |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Fox unit      | Byonics MicroFox (MF-15 or MF-PC), 1 deployed  |
| Callsign      | K7LED                                          |
| TX frequency  | 147.42 MHz (confirm for this hunt)             |
| Cycle         | 15 s on / 15 s off                             |
| CW ID         | K7LED at 20 WPM                                |

> Deploy only ONE fox until units are reprogrammed for a multi-fox
> format (both are currently programmed identically). For a mobile hunt,
> verify the duty cycle and power suit a longer drive-in search.

**Higher-power beacon:** The MicroFox units are sub-1 W, which may
limit usable range depending on the finish terrain. A planned **10 W Pi
Zero-controlled fox beacon** would extend range for a mobile hunt — see
issue [#4](https://github.com/tsayles/ham-fox-hunting/issues/4).
