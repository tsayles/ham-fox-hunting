## 2. Concept — Howarth as a finish point

Howarth is small (~28 acres) — too small for a full stand-alone
practice hunt, but well suited as the **finish line and final-fox
location for a regional (car-based) hunt**. Hounds drive the region
taking bearings, converge on Everett, park, then do a short forested
hillside walk to the hidden fox, followed by a social wrap-up with
Puget Sound / Olympic / Cascade views.

- **Final on-foot "sniff" leg:** the ~0.6-mile upper-loop forest trail
  is ideal for the close-in finish after the drive.
- **Convoy parking:** three lots (north / central / south).
- **Social wrap-up:** picnic tables, restrooms, grassy field, and
  viewpoints; a reservable shelter can host a finish-line gathering.
- **Regional anchor:** Everett's north-end waterfront lets the course
  start further south/east (I-5 / Snohomish / Lake Stevens corridor)
  and end at the water — a satisfying geographic arc.

## 3. Park rules & permit requirements

Source: City of Everett Parks (everettwa.gov), 2026.

- **Hours:** open daily 6:00 AM – dusk. Confirm the finish fits before
  dusk (~7:00 PM in late September).
- **Shelter reservation / permit:** reserve via Everett Recreation,
  **425-257-8300**; apply at least 5 business days ahead, fees paid at
  booking. A permit is required for shelter gatherings.
- **No alcohol** in city parks.
- **Dogs:** leashed except the designated off-leash **north beach**;
  clean up after pets (bring your own bags).
- **Parking:** three lots; a shelter rental does NOT guarantee parking
  on busy weekends — stage arrivals and consider carpooling.

## 5. Final-fox placement & SAFETY

**Place the final fox on the UPPER forested hillside**, on or just off
the upper-loop trail, reachable on foot from the parking lots.

**Keep the final fox — and all foot traffic — AWAY from:**

- The **BNSF railroad mainline** and the **pedestrian bridge** over the
  active tracks. Do NOT route heads-down hounds toward the tracks.
- The **beach and off-leash dog area** (north beach).
- The **spiral staircase / steep descent** to the shoreline.

**Safety notes:**

- Terrain is steep with stairs — brief teams that the final leg is on
  foot and uphill; note limited accessibility.
- Set a hard recall time and a meet-back point at the finish shelter.
- Late-September dusk (~7 PM) — keep the finish window with margin.
