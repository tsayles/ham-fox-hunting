# Fox Hunt Net Control Script

A reference script for the Fox Hunt host / net control operator.
Use the sections below at each phase of the hunt. Text inside
`[ ]` brackets is a placeholder — replace with the actual value
before or during the event.

---

## Pre-Hunt Preparation Checklist

Complete these steps before the announced start time.

- [ ] Fox transmitter deployed and confirmed on the air
  (see [`foxes/README.md`](../foxes/README.md) for programming
  and field-test procedures)
- [ ] Hunt frequency confirmed and clear of other traffic
- [ ] Fox callsign and frequency double-checked
- [ ] Hunt start and end times decided and announced in advance
- [ ] "Found it" check-in procedure decided
  (e.g., call on the hunt frequency, text a phone number, sign
  the log at the hide)
- [ ] Hunt boundary or search area defined (if applicable)
- [ ] Hint policy decided (will you give hints? how often?)
- [ ] Safety briefing prepared if needed (traffic, terrain, weather)
- [ ] Finish log ready (paper or phone) to record finder callsigns
  and times

---

## Opening Script — Hunt Start

> Read on the hunt frequency at the announced start time.

---

*"[Your callsign], net control for the [Club Name] fox hunt.*

*Good [morning / afternoon / evening], everyone.*

*The hunt is now open. The fox is transmitting on [hunt
frequency] MHz. The fox callsign is [fox callsign].*

*The hunt will run until [end time]. You have [X] minutes.*

*When you find the fox, [describe the check-in procedure —
for example: 'call me on this frequency and give me your
callsign and time' or 'sign the log at the hide'].*

*[If there are boundaries:] Please stay within [describe the
boundary or search area].*

*Any questions before we begin?*

*[Pause briefly for questions.]*

*Good luck, and 73. [Your callsign], net control, standing by."*

---

## Periodic Net Check-In

> Transmit on the hunt frequency approximately every
> [10–15] minutes, or at intervals announced in the opening.
> Adjust the hint to match your hunt's hint policy.

---

*"[Your callsign], net control — periodic check-in.*

*The time is [current time]. You have [X] minutes remaining.*

*The fox is still on the air on [hunt frequency] MHz,
callsign [fox callsign].*

*[Optional hint — if hunt policy allows:]*
*'Hint: [brief hint, e.g., "the fox is within one mile of
the parking lot" or "the fox is on a paved trail"].'*

*If you have already found the fox and have not yet checked
in, please call me now.*

*[Pause briefly for late check-ins.]*

*[Your callsign], net control, back to listening."*

---

## Closing Script — Hunt End

> Read on the hunt frequency at the announced end time.

---

*"[Your callsign], net control — the [Club Name] fox hunt
is now closed. The time is [end time].*

*All stations, please stand by while I collect finish times.*

*If you found the fox and have not yet checked in, call me
now with your callsign and the time you found it.*

*[Pause for check-ins. Record callsign and time for each.]*

*[Announce results:]*
*'The fox was found by:*
*First: [callsign] at [time].*
*[Second: [callsign] at [time].]*
*[Third: [callsign] at [time].]*
*Thank you to all [N] finders.'*

*The fox was hidden at [location description — enough detail
for participants who did not find it to understand where it
was].*

*[If the fox is still in the field:] The fox is still
transmitting at [location]. Feel free to go find it for
practice — hunt is over but the fox will remain on the air
for [X] more minutes.*

*Thank you to everyone who participated today. We'll see you
at the next hunt.*

*[Your callsign], net control, clear."*

---

## Net Control Notes

- **Keep transmissions brief and clear.** Participants may
  be driving, walking, or actively taking bearings.
- **Stay on frequency.** Participants will be listening for
  your check-ins as a timing reference.
- **Fox goes silent?** If the fox stops transmitting
  unexpectedly, announce it on the net and coordinate with
  the fox hider to investigate or restart.
- **Multi-fox hunts.** If running more than one fox,
  announce each fox separately with its own frequency and
  callsign. Confirm that only the intended fox is on the air
  before the opening (see the note in `foxes/README.md`
  about running both units simultaneously).
- **Late arrivals.** If a participant joins after the
  opening, give them a brief individual briefing off to the
  side or on a simplex frequency, then return to the hunt
  channel.
- **Record keeping.** Log the hide location, start and end
  times, participant count, and finder order. This data is
  useful for planning future hunts and tracking program
  participation.
